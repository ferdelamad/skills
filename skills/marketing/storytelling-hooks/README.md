# storytelling-hooks

Write the opening line people stop scrolling for. This skill interviews you briefly to find the real story, then writes hooks for a short video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) or a written post (X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram caption).

Built for creators and small-business owners in any niche: the florist, the trainer, the developer, the esthetician. You bring the story; the skill finds the opening.

## What a storytelling hook is

The first sentence of a story, said the way you'd start telling a friend what happened:

> "I thought I was doing everything right, until launch day."

It names the kind of story that's coming (a mistake, a turning point, a decision) and holds back the details, so people want the next line. The numbers, names and twist come right after the hook, where they pay off.

## How it works

1. **A short interview.** A few quick questions, each with an example answer, to pull out the moment that matters: what happened, a real detail, what almost went wrong, and where you'll post. Already told your story? It asks one or two follow-ups. Say "just give me hooks" and it stops asking.
2. **Category suggestions.** It picks the 2–3 hook categories that fit your story and tells you why. You can also choose from the full menu below or ask for one by name ("give me contrarian hooks").
3. **Hooks built on proven sentences.** Each category has 9 hook templates. The skill keeps each template's sentence and fills it with your story in plain words, instead of inventing slogans.
4. **Draft many, show the best.** It drafts several times more hooks than it shows and checks each one: does it sound like a person starting a story, hold back the details, put the tension up front, stay true to what happened, fit the format, and pass a scroll test (would a stranger want the next sentence?). Only the 4–6 that pass reach you.
5. **A creator profile** you can paste next time (niche, audience, voice, signature stories) to skip half the questions.

## What you get

**Video hooks**
- **Hook**: the opening sentence, said out loud and shown as big text over the first frame, so it works with or without sound. About 15 words or fewer.
- **On screen** (when needed): a trimmed version of the same sentence if the full one is too long to read.
- **Opening shot**: what's on camera in the first second, something you can actually film.
- **Then**: your next line, where the details land.

**Post hooks**
- **Hook**: the first line, landing before the "...more" cutoff (about 125 characters).
- **Line 2** (optional): the first concrete detail.
- **Then**: the line that starts paying it off.

## A session

> **You:** Write me a hook for a reel. A client cried in my chair last week. She'd been hiding her skin under her hair for years.
>
> **Skill:** Two quick things. What did she say or do that told you something had changed? And is this for Instagram Reels or TikTok?
>
> **You:** She asked me to take her photo. Instagram.
>
> **Skill:** This fits **Client/Customer** (her photo request is the whole story) and **Turning Point** (the moment she stopped hiding).
>
> **1. Client/Customer · Reel**
> - **Hook**: "A client cried in my chair last week, and it wasn't because it hurt."
> - **On screen**: "She cried in my chair. Not because it hurt."
> - **Opening shot**: your hands clipping a client's hair back from her face
> - **Then**: "She'd hidden her cheeks under her hair for years. That day she asked me for a photo."

## 10 examples

Ten creators, ten categories. Each hook keeps a proven sentence and holds the details for the next line.

**1. Esthetician · Client/Customer · Reel**
Story: a client who'd hidden her skin under her hair for years cried after her treatment and asked for a photo.
- **Hook**: "A client cried in my chair last week, and it wasn't because it hurt."
- **Then**: "She'd hidden her cheeks under her hair for years. That day she asked me for a photo."

**2. Florist · Curiosity · Instagram post**
Story: a man ordering a 40th birthday bouquet didn't know his wife's favourite colour, so he called his daughter from the shop.
- **Hook**: A man asked me for a 40th birthday bouquet. Then he couldn't answer my first question.
- **Then**: The question was her favourite colour. He called his daughter right there at my counter.

**3. Personal trainer · Contrarian · X post**
Story: every gym he worked at sold 6-day plans; his clients train three 30-minute sessions a week, and his longest client has stayed 14 months.
- **Hook**: Everyone told me to sell 6-day plans. I did the opposite.
- **Then**: My clients train three times a week, 30 minutes each. My longest client has been with me 14 months.

**4. Solo developer · Mistake · Reel**
Story: 8 months building an app alone, 3 signups on launch day, then 40 messages to a competitor's users that revealed the missing feature.
- **Hook**: "I thought I was doing everything right, until launch day."
- **Opening shot**: the analytics dashboard, user count reading 3
- **Then**: "I'd spent eight months building it, and three people signed up. So I messaged 40 people using my competitor's app."

**5. Coffee shop owner · Decision · LinkedIn post**
Story: turned down the shop's biggest-ever wholesale order because the buyer wanted cheaper beans.
- **Hook**: I said no to our biggest wholesale order ever, for one reason.
- **Then**: They wanted us to switch to cheaper beans. Our regulars would have tasted it within a week.

**6. Freelance designer · Lesson · Threads post**
Story: ten years of client work taught her to cap revisions in every contract.
- **Hook**: After 10 years of client work, here's what I know for sure.
- **Then**: "Just one more round" is never one more round. Every contract I send now caps revisions at two.

**7. Real estate agent · Turning Point · TikTok**
Story: about to quit in his second year, a buyer he'd lost six months earlier called back and became his first referral source.
- **Hook**: "I almost quit real estate in my second year, until one phone call."
- **Opening shot**: his phone lighting up on the desk with an unknown number
- **Then**: "A buyer I'd lost six months earlier called back. She's sent me nine clients since."

**8. Hairstylist · Behind-the-Scenes · Reel**
Story: a "simple trim" that took two appointments, because the first one was spent listening to what the client actually wanted.
- **Hook**: "This looks like a simple trim. It took two appointments."
- **Opening shot**: the finished cut, then a hard cut to the first appointment, no scissors in hand
- **Then**: "The first appointment, I didn't cut anything. I just listened."

**9. Nutrition coach · Myth-buster · Instagram post**
Story: she cut carbs for years, felt worse, and only made progress when she started eating them again with protein at every meal.
- **Hook**: I believed carbs were the problem for six years. My own food diary proved me wrong.
- **Then**: The weeks I cut them were the weeks I binged by Friday.

**10. Candle shop founder · Transformation · LinkedIn post**
Story: went from packing orders on her kitchen floor to a small studio unit, and still packs the first order of every day herself.
- **Hook**: A year ago I was packing orders on my kitchen floor. Today, here's where I am.
- **Then**: We moved into a 400 sq ft studio last month. I still pack the first order of every day myself.

## The 15 hook categories

| Category | Use it when | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Transformation** | You're sharing a journey: where you started, where you are, what moved you. | "A year ago I was packing orders on my kitchen floor. Today, here's where I am." |
| **Mistake** | A failure, a wrong call or an expensive lesson. | "I thought I was doing everything right, until launch day." |
| **Turning Point** | Something changed your thinking, your business or your approach. | "I almost quit real estate in my second year, until one phone call." |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | You're showing the work, decisions and details people never see. | "This looks like a simple trim. It took two appointments." |
| **Contrarian** | Your experience goes against the usual advice in your field. | "Everyone told me to sell 6-day plans. I did the opposite." |
| **Relatable** | You want to connect through a struggle your audience knows too well. | "If you've ever felt guilty charging a no-show fee, I get it." |
| **Client/Customer** | A real moment, result or lesson from working with someone. | "A client cried in my chair last week, and it wasn't because it hurt." |
| **Curiosity** | You want to open a loop that makes people need the next line. | "There's one question I ask every client before I touch their skin." |
| **Lesson** | You're turning experience into one piece of useful advice. | "After 10 years of client work, here's what I know for sure." |
| **Decision** | A hard choice, a risk, or the moment you chose differently. | "I said no to our biggest wholesale order ever, for one reason." |
| **Myth-buster** | You're correcting a belief people in your niche get wrong. | "I believed carbs were the problem for six years. My own food diary proved me wrong." |
| **Number/List** | Your story packs into a count or a timeframe. | "3 things I ask every first-time client before we book." |
| **Question** | You want your audience to answer, especially in written posts. | "Would you pay more for your coffee if you knew who grew it?" |
| **Origin** | You're telling why you started, or why you do it your way. | "I never planned to open a flower shop. Then a friend asked me to do her wedding." |
| **Credibility** | Your experience, volume or milestones are the hook. | "After 1,200 weddings, here's what most couples get wrong about flowers." |

Each category has 9 templates in [`hooks.en.md`](hooks.en.md). The skill fills them with your story, so you never get a hook with a blank left in it.
