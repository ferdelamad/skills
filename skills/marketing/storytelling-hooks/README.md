# storytelling-hooks

Writes the opening hook for a short video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) or a written post (X, Threads, LinkedIn, Instagram caption). Built for creators and small-business owners in any niche.

## What it does

1. Asks a few short questions to find the real story (or skips them if you already told it, or say "just give me hooks").
2. Suggests 2–3 hook categories that fit your story, with a reason for each. You can also pick from all 15 or name one.
3. Writes 4–6 hooks. Video hooks come in three layers: what you say, the on-screen text, and the first-frame visual. Post hooks come as a first line that lands before the "...more" cutoff.
4. Checks every hook: it has to use a real detail from your story, carry some tension, fit the format, and be a promise your story can keep.
5. Offers a short creator profile you can paste next time to skip half the questions.

## Files

- `SKILL.md`: the workflow.
- `hooks.en.md`: the hook library, with 15 categories and 8–10 fill-in-the-blank templates each.

## Install

**Claude Code (plugin):** see the [repo README](../../../README.md#installation).

**Claude Code (manual):** copy this folder into `~/.claude/skills/` (global) or `.claude/skills/` (per project).

**Claude app:** zip this folder (with `storytelling-hooks/` at the root of the zip) and upload it under Settings > Capabilities > Skills.
